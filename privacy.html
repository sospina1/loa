LOA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

LOA is an emotional wellbeing companion app. This policy explains what data it collects, where that data goes, and how you can delete it. It is written to be understood, not to cover us.

LOA is not a medical or psychological service. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it is not a substitute for care from a health professional. If you are in danger or in crisis, seek human help: the app includes a directory of helplines and a button to call a person you trust.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of this data is Sebastián Ospina, developer of the LOA app (com.sospina.aicompanion).

Contact: sospinagaviria@gmail.com

2. What data is collected

What you write or choose

  • Conversations with the app's companion.
  • Journal entries and mood check-ins.
  • Your profile: name or nickname, what you prefer to be called, what you tell the app about yourself, your goals and the topics you tend to bring up.
  • Memories: facts the app saves about you from your conversations, so it doesn't start from zero every time. You can see and delete them one by one from the Memory screen.
  • Trusted contact: if you choose to add one, another person's name and phone number, for the call button in the crisis notice. That person did not install the app — only save them if they agree.
  • Preferences: theme, language, text size, reminders, country for the helpline directory.

What is generated automatically

  • An anonymous identifier, created the first time you open the app. No email or sign-up is required to use it.
  • Your Google account, only if you choose to connect it to use the app on more than one device. In that case Firebase stores your Google email and name; the app does not copy them anywhere else.
  • A notification identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging token) so the app can send you reminders.
  • Your device's advertising identifier and basic technical data, if you see ads. See section 4.

The microphone

If you use voice mode or dictate a message, the app asks for microphone permission and uses your Android device's speech recognition. LOA does not record or store audio: it only receives the resulting text. The processing is done by your phone's speech recognition service, which on most devices is Google's and may send the audio to its servers. That is governed by Google's privacy policy and by your device settings.

What is NOT collected

  • No usage analytics and no behavioural tracking of our own.
  • No crash reporting that sends data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files or location.
  • No data is sold to anyone. Ever.

3. Where it goes and who it is shared with

Every provider involved is Google. There are no third parties beyond the ones listed here.

Important, about the companion's replies. LOA currently uses the no-cost tier of the Gemini API. Under that service's terms, Google may use the content submitted and the responses generated to improve and develop its products, and human reviewers may read it (disconnected from the account and the API key).

Put plainly: what you write in the chat may be read by people at Google. Keep that in mind when deciding what to share. We plan to move to a service tier where this does not happen; when we do, this policy will be updated and the date above will show it.

4. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads, plus an optional rewarded ad if you want to unlock an extra session or a few more messages. Ads never appear in the chat, during a meditation or breathing session, in voice mode, or while you are writing in the journal.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised advertising, and you can change your mind at any time from Settings → Data → Ads privacy.

With a Premium subscription, no ads are shown at all.

5. How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Data is kept for as long as you use the app. There is no automatic deletion after inactivity.

You can delete:

  • A memory, from the Memory screen.
  • A conversation, from the history.
  • Everything, from Settings → Data → Delete all my data. This removes what is stored on the phone and what is stored in the cloud under your identifier. It cannot be undone.

Deleting everything does not cancel an active subscription — that is done from Google Play. It also does not delete what has already been sent to the Gemini API, which is governed by the Google terms described above.

6. Security

Communication with every service is encrypted (HTTPS). In the cloud, Firestore security rules prevent one person's content from being accessible to another: each person can only read and write in their own space.

No system is infallible, and this policy does not claim otherwise.

7. Minimum age

LOA is for people 18 and over. The artificial intelligence technology it uses carries that age restriction in its terms of service, and this app deals with subjects that call for adult judgement. If you are under 18, do not use it.

8. Your rights

You can access your data from within the app, correct it, delete it, and stop using the service whenever you want, without giving reasons. If you live somewhere with specific data protection law — such as the European GDPR or Law 1581 of 2012 in Colombia — you can exercise your rights by writing to the contact address in section 1.

9. Changes to this policy

If anything relevant changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will also be announced inside the app.
